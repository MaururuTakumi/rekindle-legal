Common questions

How do I hear stories in my own voice? During onboarding, choose the voice step and record about 15 seconds. You can also skip it and use the narrator voice.

Where is my data? Your answers and stories are stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Premium? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period.
