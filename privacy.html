Privacy Policy

App: On His Mind: SP Manifestation (in-app name: Rekindle) · Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Operator: Honkoma Co., Ltd.

Rekindle creates short, personalized audio stories from details you provide about yourself and a person who matters to you. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information you provide

During onboarding you may enter your name, your person's name, details about your relationship, memories, and wishes. This information is stored on your device and is used to generate your stories. The app has no user accounts and we do not operate a server that stores your stories or profile.

Voice recordings

If you choose the "your own voice" option, the app records a short voice sample with your permission. The recording is stored on your device. To create a narration voice from it, the sample is sent to our voice provider (ElevenLabs) over an encrypted connection, which returns a voice model identifier that the app keeps on your device. If you skip this option, no recording is made and no voice data leaves your device.

AI processing

To write your stories and produce their narration, the details you entered (and, for narration in your cloned voice, the text of the story) are sent over encrypted connections to our AI providers (including OpenAI, Google, and ElevenLabs). They process the content only to return the story or audio to your device. We do not sell your information, and your content is not used for advertising.

What we do not collect

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

If premium features are offered, payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Data retention and deletion

Your stories, answers, and recordings live on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Voice models created with our voice provider can be deleted on request — contact us and we will remove the voice model associated with your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
